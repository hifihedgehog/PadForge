# Razer controller skins

These custom recreations use the shared Carbon Xbox Series mesh, control textures, moving decals, and clear button covers. Each appearance supplies a 4096x4096 Shell.png for the faceplate, rear shell, grip backs, and battery cover.

| BobaFett | [Razer Boba Fett product photos](https://www.windowscentral.com/razer-launches-boba-fett-xbox-wireless-controller-and-charging-stand) |
| Mandalorian | [Mandalorian Beskar front](https://9to5toys.com/2021/11/16/razer-mandalorian-beskar-controller/) and [rear product photo](https://www.cinelinx.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Razer-Controller-and-Quick-Charging-Stand_for-Xbox-BACK_Mandalorian_Beskar-Edition-scaled.jpg) |

Squadrons adapts the older Xbox One finish to PadForge's shared Xbox Series model. Razer's translucent green finish uses an opaque painted approximation of the visible interior. The standard model battery cover keeps its original geometry.
