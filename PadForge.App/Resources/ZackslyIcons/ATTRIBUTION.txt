Modifications in PadForge
-------------------------
PadForge ships derivative tab icons in Resources/ControllerIcons.xaml
that re-use the outer body geometry of the stick and trigger SVGs
(outer ring + inner disc for sticks; outer trigger silhouette for
triggers). The originals' lettering is not used; per-section labels
(L/R/LT/RT/L2/R2/digits) are rendered at runtime by
Views/Controls/LabeledShapeIcon as a Path built via
FormattedText.BuildGeometry against Bahnschrift SemiBold Condensed
(a Windows system font), composited over the licensed body shape.
