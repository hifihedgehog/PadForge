Press a button on your controller. Browsers hide a controller until you press something on it. A pad paired to this phone, or the pad built into this handheld, then shows here and PadForge sees it as a controller on the PC.
Two or more pads on one device each become their own controller. Extra buttons beyond the standard seventeen appear on the paddle and Misc slots, up to ten, and you map them like any other button. A pad the browser did not recognize is forwarded raw: up to 21 buttons and six axes, each axis as the browser reports it. PadForge notes where each axis sat when the pad connected and returns it there if the page goes quiet. Rumble goes to the pad when its browser can drive it, otherwise to the phone's vibrator where the browser offers one. iPhone Safari offers neither, so a pad forwarded from an iPhone gets no rumble. Each pad's row says where its rumble goes. PadForge checks in with this page once a second. If the page stops answering for a few seconds, PadForge releases everything on the pad and ignores the pad until the page answers again.
